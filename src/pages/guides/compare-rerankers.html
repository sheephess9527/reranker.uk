Best reranker in 2026: Qwen3, bge, mxbai, Jina, GTE and the hosted APIs compared

Comparison · ~7 min read · Updated 10 Nov 2026

Every reranker vendor publishes a table in which its own model wins, and the tables use different datasets, so the numbers cannot be lined up across vendors. This page only sets scores side by side when they come from one run, says whose run it was, and says plainly which models have no number that can be compared.

The short answer

  • Self-hosting on a GPU and want the best open model: Qwen3-Reranker-4B. In both runs below it is at or near the top, and it is Apache 2.0.
  • Small, CPU-friendly, multilingual: bge-reranker-v2-m3 is the standard baseline; the GTE rerankers are smaller still, and Hindsight’s hs-rerank-1 (MIT, {{fact:hs-rerank-1.params}}) is smaller again but comes with no scores we can quote.
  • No GPU and no ops: a hosted API. Cohere Rerank 5 and Voyage rerank-3 are the established ones, zerank-2 the cheapest per token.
  • Commercial product, open weights: check the licence first. Jina’s weights are non-commercial.

Which numbers can be compared

A score only means something next to scores from the same run: the same datasets, the same candidate lists from the same first-stage retriever, the same cut-off. Two numbers from two vendors’ pages are not on one scale, even when both say “nDCG@10” or “BEIR”. This page uses two runs that cover several models at once, and both were run by a vendor with a model of its own in the table, so read them as strong hints and confirm on your own labelled queries (how).

Three families have no run to put here. GTE: Alibaba publishes its reranking results as a chart, so there is no table to line up. Cohere: no benchmark table has been published for Rerank 5. hs-rerank-1: Hindsight’s model card shows its results as an image only. They are compared below on size, licence, price and fit instead.

How to decide

  1. Pick two candidates from this page: one open model you could host and one hosted API.
  2. Label 30–50 real queries from your own users with the passages that answer them.
  3. Run both and compare nDCG@10 on your labels, with the same candidate list. This is the only comparison that predicts your result; the evaluation guide walks through it.
  4. Check that reranking is the bottleneck. If the right passage is never in the candidates, no reranker helps; see why reranking didn’t help.
