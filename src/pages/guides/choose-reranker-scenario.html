Which reranker fits your scenario?

Decision guide · ~8 min read · Updated 12 Oct 2026

Rerankers are not one-size-fits-all. The right choice depends on language coverage, latency budget, data sensitivity, and whether your queries look more like support tickets, legal clauses, code search, or RAG chunks.

RAG & knowledge bases

Retrieve 50–100 chunks, rerank to 5–10, then prompt. Without a GPU, bge-reranker-v2-m3 is the usual self-hosted default; with one, Qwen3-Reranker-4B, which in Qwen’s own tests beats the 8B on English. As a hosted API, Cohere Rerank 5 and Voyage rerank-3 both bill per token, at the same list price — the cost calculator compares them with the other APIs at your volume. See rerank for RAG and hybrid retrieval when lexical matches matter.

Rule of thumb: if bi-encoder recall is already good, a mid-size reranker is enough. If recall is messy, fix retrieval width first.

Chinese and multilingual

The classic small models — ms-marco MiniLM, Jina v1, mxbai v1 — were trained on English only. Qwen3-Reranker (100+ languages), bge-reranker-v2-m3 and the 306M gte-multilingual-reranker-base (70+ languages) were trained for it. If your servers are in mainland China and you would rather not host a model, Alibaba Cloud Model Studio sells qwen3-rerank by the token in yuan, and SiliconFlow serves bge-reranker-v2-m3 and Qwen3-Reranker behind one endpoint; both are in the models table and the cost calculator’s yuan table. To download open weights in mainland China, hf-mirror.com mirrors Hugging Face. The mainland China guide compares the three routes in detail.

Customer support

Queries are short, passages are FAQ-sized, and latency dominates UX. A tiny cross-encoder (Jina v1 tiny, ms-marco MiniLM) often suffices for English — you are choosing among a handful of similar answers, not reading 100-page PDFs. For other languages, start with bge-reranker-v2-m3. Try the Customer support preset in our demo.

Legal & compliance

Distractors share vocabulary (“termination”, “notice period”, “breach”). Bi-encoders confuse them; cross-encoders separate nuance, and an instruction-following reranker lets you say what counts — “the clause that governs termination for convenience”, “later amendments supersede earlier ones”. Qwen3-Reranker and Voyage rerank-3 take instructions; Contextual AI Rerank v2’s weights are non-commercial. Budget extra latency for a larger model and keep passages to single clauses. Demo preset: Legal clause.

Code & technical docs

Vector search alone misses exact function names; BM25 alone misses paraphrased “how do I…” questions. Use hybrid retrieval, then rerank. Qwen publish code-retrieval scores for Qwen3-Reranker (see the Qwen page), and Voyage report rerank-3’s gain over rerank-2.5 on their code datasets (see the Voyage page). Demo presets: Technical docs and Code search.

Scanned PDFs, slides and screenshots

If your documents are page images — scans, slide decks, dashboards — a text reranker only sees whatever OCR extracted. Qwen3-VL-Reranker (2B / 8B) and LightOn-rerank score page images directly, both under Apache 2.0, and Alibaba Cloud sells qwen3-vl-rerank as an API. NVIDIA’s Nemotron-3.5-Rerank-8B and the smaller llama-nemotron-rerank-vl-1b-v2 do the same under licences that allow commercial use, and take a page’s image and its extracted text together. Their published scores are on ViDoRe, a visual-document benchmark, so compare them with each other rather than with the BEIR column.
