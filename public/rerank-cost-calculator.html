Hosted rerankers do not bill the same way. Cohere charges per search — one query plus up to 100 documents, with any document over 500 tokens counted as several. Voyage charges per token. The two land closer than they look, and which is cheaper depends on how your passages round. Put your own numbers in.

Why the ranking changes

Both vendors end up charging for text, but they round it differently. Cohere bills each candidate as one document per started 500 tokens — query included — and each search covers 100 documents. Voyage bills exactly the tokens you send, counting the query once per document.

At list prices the two meet exactly: 500 tokens costs the same on Cohere Rerank 4 Pro ($0.0025 per search of 100 documents) and Voyage rerank-3 ($0.05 per 1M tokens) — Voyage’s own pricing page uses that very example. What separates them is rounding:

  • Short passages — Cohere still bills each one as a full document, so per-token billing wins, by more the shorter they are.
  • Just past a 500-token boundary — a 520-token candidate is two documents on Cohere; per-token billing costs about half.
  • Top-k that isn’t a multiple of 100 — Cohere rounds each query up to a whole search, so cost steps up rather than sliding.
  • Cohere Fast vs Voyage rerank-3 — Fast is cheaper only when query plus passage fills more than 80% of its last chunk. The -lite tier is cheaper than both on any workload.

Correction, October 2026: until now this page said Cohere’s 500-token splitting was a myth and that per-search pricing ignores passage length. Cohere’s own pricing FAQ says otherwise: a document over 500 tokens, counting the query, is split, and every chunk counts toward the 100 documents in a search. The calculator now models that, which removes the long-passage advantage the old version showed for Cohere.

What about self-hosting?

Self-hosting has no per-call price, so it does not belong in the table above — you trade a usage bill for a GPU bill plus operations. The comparison only becomes meaningful at your own volume: divide your monthly GPU cost by the number of queries above and compare that to the per-query figures in the table. Below a few hundred thousand queries a month an API is usually cheaper than a dedicated GPU; well above that, self-hosting a Qwen3-Reranker or bge-reranker-v2-m3 starts to pay off. Our self-hosting guide covers the serving side.

Note that a smaller model is not automatically a worse one — Jina Reranker v3.5 reaches 63.20 BEIR nDCG@10 at 0.6B in Jina's own test, level with a 4B model, so the cheapest thing to serve may also be among the most accurate.

Assumptions and sources

  • Cohere Rerank 4 — $0.0025 per search (Pro) and $0.002 per search (Fast). A search is one query plus up to 100 documents; per Cohere’s pricing FAQ, a document longer than 500 tokens including the query is split, and each chunk counts as a document. Prices are from cohere.com/pricing ($2.50 and $2 per 1,000 searches).
  • Voyage rerank-3 — $0.05 per 1M tokens, and $0.02 for rerank-3-lite. Billable tokens are the query counted once per document, plus every document token you send — so 50 candidates means the query is billed 50 times. The older rerank-2.5 and -lite cost the same per token. Free tokens and the Batch API discount are not applied here — see the Voyage page for which models they cover.
  • Token counts are approximate — every vendor tokenises differently, and this page multiplies your averages rather than tokenising real text.
  • Rates verified 1 October 2026 and re-checked against Cohere and Voyage daily by this site’s source check. Confirm before committing to a budget.
